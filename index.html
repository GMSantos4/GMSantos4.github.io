Object Recognition in Images with Convolutional Neural Networks

Transfer learning and fine-tuning of VGG16, Xception and ResNet101 to classify drones, cars, ships and airplanes, with data augmentation and evaluation by confusion matrix, precision, recall and F1-score. Xception reached accuracy and F1-score above 98%.

Finite Element Analysis of a Bulk Carrier Midship Section

Longitudinal stress analysis of the China Steel Liberty midship section in Ansys Mechanical, under hogging and sagging wave bending moments from the IACS Common Structural Rules, with a mesh convergence study and comparison against classical ship beam theory.
